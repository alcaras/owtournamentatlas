# Old World Community Tournament — Rules (Essentials)

This is a 1v1 community event: competitive, but first about enjoying Old World
together. Players may mutually agree to change any **non-bold** setting; **bold**
settings are fixed. Anything not covered here is resolved by Organizers in the
spirit of fair, welcoming play (the Golden Rule). Live network games: **UNDO OFF**.

**Organizers:** Alcaras, Becked, FluffyBunny, Siontific. All coordination on the
Old World Discord.

## 1. Structure

- **Swiss Qualifier** — 5 rounds, two timezone divisions (Western/US, Eastern/EU).
  Win 3 → Championship Bracket. Lose 3 → eliminated. 3 games min, 5 max.
- **Championship Bracket** — global single-elim, ~4–5 rounds to Grand Finals.
  Seeded from Qualifier standings.
- **Tiebreakers (Swiss → bracket seeding):** 1) record, 2) Buchholz (sum of
  opponents' wins), 3) head-to-head, 4) Organizer ruling.
- **Substitutes** — Swiss only, inherit the replaced player's W/L record.

## 2. Ruleset

Map: assigned before match · **Map Size: Duel** · Map Aspect: Square ·
Nation/Leader: Pick Later · Pick Archetype · No Ancient Ruins · **Mirror Map** ·
**Point Symmetry** · Tribal Strength: Normal · Forced March: Double Fatigue ·
**Victories: Points + Time (Double & Ambition OFF)** · **Points to Win: High** ·
Unique Nations · Starting Techs On · **Do Not Show Critical Hits** · Calamities:
Rare · Events: Moderate · **Competitive Mode** · Prosperity: Modest · **All DLC** ·
**Turn Timer: Slow** · Turn Style: Tight (Simultaneous before contact) ·
Observers: On.

Bold = non-negotiable. Both players need a copy of the base game (any platform,
crossplay OK); tell Organizers before the match if you lack any DLC.

**Version lock:** the season runs on the patch announced at sign-up. If a patch
drops mid-round, in-progress games finish on their started build; new games use
the locked build unless Organizers say otherwise.

## 3. Maps & Rerolls

Maps target: capitals land-connected, ~18–22 city sites, ~50-hex land distance.
**Objective reroll (either player may call, no agreement needed):** no land
connection between capitals; capitals effectively adjacent; wildly off site
count. **Mutual reroll:** any other map both players agree is bad (e.g. walled
in by mountains). Not for "didn't get the resources I wanted."

## 4. Turns & Timers

Start in **Simultaneous**; switch to **Tight** on first contact between nations.
All play uses a timer (slow standard; faster only by mutual consent; first turn
untimed). Allowed in Tight: civilian moves, reactive build/queue/tech changes,
promotions, generals, healing, anchoring. Discouraged: civilian body-blocks
during active conflict. **Scout hunting** during Simultaneous is fair — keep
your scouts safe. Pauses are allowed for breaks/disputes; abusing pause/banked
time to plan is cheating.

## 5. Scheduling, Results & Disruptions

- Players schedule promptly at earliest mutual convenience; **round deadline:
  [X] days**. No-show grace **15 min**, then forfeit. Unscheduled by deadline →
  Organizer adjudication (double-loss or coin-flip).
- **Result reporting:** winner reports in Discord within 24h with final-save or
  screenshot proof.
- A match ends at the point threshold or by surrender/concession. Play on while
  morale holds; concede gracefully when decisively beaten. Optional Post-Game
  Chat on stream is encouraged.
- **Unfinished games:** hard cap **[X] hours / turn N]**; if reached, result by
  points then Organizer ruling.
- **Tech failure:** alert Organizers; allow 30 min for the disconnected player;
  otherwise save and reschedule. Saves are kept by [host/Organizer].

## 6. Coverage

Matches should be open to observers for casting. Players need not arrange or wait
on streamers — **scheduling beats streaming**. By participating you consent to
live broadcast and perpetual video use.

## 7. Conduct & Violations

Be respectful; honor names/pronouns; no harassment or hateful conduct. Escalation
ladder: warning → game forfeit → tournament removal/ban, at Organizer discretion;
appeals go to Organizers.

**Cheating includes:** bypassing rule intent; configs that unfairly favor one
player; collusion; reading save/game files for opponent info; outside strategy
help; watching your live match/VOD/chat before it ends. Players may use the OW
Discord during a match (non-strategic only). Streamer-players: disable chat
during live play; enable comments on VODs only after the match ends.

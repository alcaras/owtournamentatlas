## **Old World Community Tournament Rules**

Welcome\! This tournament is an event that we host in order to build up the community around Old World. It is as much about having a great time and enjoying each other’s company as it is competition and showing off good play and cool strategies; our hope is that the foes you meet on the battlefield will become friends you can play with regularly for months or even years to come.  
Old World is a complex strategy game with many different ways to play. We started these tournaments to get people playing and talking about the game so that we can learn from each other and expand our collective knowledge of the game, all while finding people we can play with consistently. The spirit of competition is important, but at the end of the day this event is a celebration of the game we love.

The guiding principle of all tournament games is that, ultimately, the players have fun. Many format and gameplay settings of a given match are subject to the discretion of its players; if both players in a matchup agree on something, then they are welcome to make adjustments for their game where applicable. In this document we primarily offer guidance for setting up games and how we believe players should behave, and in some places we have strict rules to make sure everyone's time and comfort is respected.

This document is intended to help you get started in the tournament and establishes some guidelines that we follow when it comes to organizing tournament matches. Our hope is to ensure that the tournament happens in a responsible amount of time, is both fun and fair, and expands the Old World community.  
   **The Golden Rule**

First and foremost \- this tournament is meant to create a welcoming atmosphere for players to enjoy Old World Multiplayer. Any behavior not specifically outlined in this document that is judged by tournament organizers to go against the spirit of the rules expressed herein, may be subjected to a ruling from organizers in order to maintain the integrity and enjoyment of this event.

**2.3 Tournament Structure**  
The tournament will be played in through two Phases: a Swiss Qualifying round and an Championship Bracket. As this is an international tournament, players who progress should expect to navigate scheduling games across different time zones.  
**Swiss Qualifier:** This phase will be open to all players who sign up for the tournament before the stated deadline. The Swiss Qualifiers will be split into two divisions that roughly correspond within a range of timezones: the Western Division (US) and the Eastern Division (Europe). Players can indicate their preference at the time of sign-up. Though some players may not receive their preferred division because of the need to fill rosters appropriately, Organizers will endeavor to assign players according to their request.

 The Swiss Qualifier phase will consist of 5 rounds of play; in the first round, players are paired with an opponent and a map script, and the pick order preference will be determined before the match begins. In round two, players who won their first match are all paired against each other, as are players who lost their first match.  
Pairing continues this way as the rounds advance until every player in the tournament has either won or lost three games. Players who win three games advance into the championship bracket, while players who lose three games are eliminated from the tournament.  
The minimum amount of expected games to be played in this portion of the tournament for all players is three games. The maximum amount possible is 5 games.

 **Championship Bracket:** The Championship Bracket phase consists of a series of single-elimination rounds in order to determine the tournament champion. The pool and seeding will be determined by the Qualifiers.  
The Championship Bracket will not have divisions and will be paired globally; Initial seeding will involve taking players from the qualifiers and pairing the top rated player from one division against the lowest rated players from the other division. Players must win each match to advance up the bracket; a single loss leads to being eliminated from the tournament.  
This phase of the tournament will consist of approximately 4-5 rounds, with the final round being the Grand Finals: pitting the last two remaining players against each other to determine the tournament champion.

**2.4 Scheduling Your Game**  
Tournament communications will be managed through the Discord app; therefore, all players must have an account.. When players are paired against each other, Tournament Organizers will use the Discord app to create a group chat between the players in order to facilitate game scheduling and ensure players are attempting to organize their match.  
The responsibility of setting a date and time that works for both players is ultimately left to the players themselves; we ask that players be considerate of the time commitments of their opponents and do their best to compromise to ensure games continue at a reasonable pace without delay.  
**2.5 Video Coverage**  
It is expected that all matches be open to observers for the purpose of organizing a Host Streamer to cast the game to be viewed by spectators. Players are **not** responsible for organizing a host streamer for their game, nor are they expected to plan their game around the availability of streamers. Priority should always be given to scheduling and playing games over the ability to record and stream them. We ask that games not be delayed due to streaming reasons. Tournament Organizers and Streamers will do their best to ensure the broadest possible amount of coverage for as many games in the tournament as possible.

By participating in this tournament you consent to be featured in live broadcasts in an appropriate manner and, for each match to be used in video content in perpetuity. We appreciate our players immensely as they make capturing all of the action possible\!

**2.6  Map Rerolls**  
If both players agree the map sucks \-- they can mutually agree to reroll. We've had a few mapgens that haven't been great (though we also admire players who stick with it and push through). So it's left to player discretion. We anticipate this being used for situations like both players being walled off by mountains, etc. not "I didn't get the resources I wanted in my starting location."

#### **2.7 Game Length**

#### **Old World Tournament games vary in length depending on many factors; map generation, game settings, player skill, availability, and even luck. A game could take as little as 2-3 hours to finish or as many as 10+ hours if no player can gain a decisive advantage. The average game between players will likely take 2-3 scheduled sessions of play, and it is therefore recommended that players schedule about 2-3 hours of play per session if possible.**

#### **2.8 Victory and End-of-Match Procedure**

#### **A match will conclude automatically when one player reaches the point threshold to achieve victory for that game. The other method of victory is through conquest, which usually results in the surrender of one player once their empire is considered defeated decisively, this is not the same as a full conquest victory, but usually the result of losing a massive battle, the loss of crucial territory, or the realization that there is simply not enough time to contest the point victory of your opponent. Players are encouraged to play on for as long as their morale might hold, but it should be understood that in many cases, matches will conclude by one player conceding defeat to their opponent. If your game concludes live on stream, players will be invited and encouraged to join a “Post Game Chat” to discuss the events of their game with the casters and audience. This is optional, but highly encouraged and greatly appreciated by viewers.**

**2.9 Technological Errors and Delays**  
Should a technological error occur, such as an internet disconnection or a power outage, Tournament Organizers should be immediately alerted if possible. Players should give at least 30 minutes for a disconnected player to communicate whether or not they expect their technological issue to be resolved. If the game can be paused and continued the same day, players are encouraged to organize a time to return to the game. However, if the issue cannot be resolved, the game should be saved and a new session rescheduled.

**3\. Turn styles and Timers**

#### **Old World is, strictly speaking, a turn-based game. However, due to the realities and frictions of Real World time constraints, this tournament is primarily played using the Tight turn style (detailed in section 3.2) in order to help facilitate faster gameplay. Players can decide how much or how little to use Simultaneous play, but due to time constraints, tournament play will be played in Tight with a Slow Timer.**

#### 

#### **3.1 Simultaneous Play**

#### Player turns start at the same time, and all units and actions can be done by both players. This is most common at the start of matches, where early exploration and expansion does not often involve player conflict. The following are some guidelines to help set expectations of conduct around simultaneous play:

* Civilian moves (scouts, workers, settlers) are allowed.  
* Aggressive military moves against the human player should be avoided until players agree to swap to tight.  
* **Scout Hunting: There may be opportunities where scouts are vulnerable to military units during simultaneous play; players are expected to keep their scouts safe during this era, and it is generally viewed as acceptable for an opponent to take an opportunistic attack to try and remove a scout from play.**

**3.2 Tight Turns**  
When playing in Tight, players are able to do many different actions on their opponents turn that they would not normally be able to do. This includes but is not limited to; moving civilian units (scouts, workers, etc), changing research or build queues, assigning characters or promotions, etc. A consequence of this mode is that some things are able to happen out of sequence. For example, a defending player will always be able to repair their city as soon as it becomes under attack. Here are some guidelines to help set expectations of conduct around tight play:

* Civilian moves (scouts, workers, settlers) are allowed.

* Reactive Build queue changing (Repairs, Unit rushing, removing items from que) are allowed.  
* Swapping Technologies is allowed.  
* Civilian Body-blocks / “military” moves during active conflict are discouraged; I.e. if you left a city undefended, avoid moving a worker in to defend the city while it comes under attack on your opponents turns.  
* Promotions, Generals, Upgrades, Healing, Anchoring on opponents turns are all acceptable.

**3.2 Transitioning from Simultaneous to Tight**  
Players will transition from Simultaneous play to Tight turns when their nations make contact with each other for the first time.   
**3.3 Timers**  
All tournament play must be played using a timer. Standard rules require a slow timer, but players may negotiate a faster one if necessary. The first turn of a game need not be played with a timer.  
 Breaks during play are permitted, and players may pause the timer. Additionally if there is any issue that requires the players to discuss the game amongst themselves or with tournament organizers, the timer may be paused. If Streamers notice an issue that requires bringing to the attention of the players, they may pause the game on the players behalf. Abusing the timer and the privilege of pausing in order to gain more time to plan or prepare your turn is considered a form of cheating.  
Each player has an amount of “Banked” time that they may use to increase the length of their turns in order to take more time for decision making. To use this time, press the hourglass icon in the top left corner of the U.I. near the pause button.

**4\. Tournament Violations**  
In the interest of good sportsmanship and fair play, we ask players to engage respectfully in ways that may not explicitly be highlighted by these rules. When in doubt as to if your behavior goes against the spirit outlined in these rules, it is best to ask and be informed than to assume that something not explicitly stated is fair play. In the event a conflict arises, the Tournament Organizers reserve the right to make judgements and rulings on behavior that may not be outlined in these rules in the interest of keeping the tournament fair and welcoming for everyone.

By participating in this tournament, all players agree to abide by the rules outlined in this document, as well as the spirit and intent expressed herein. Players agree to accommodate any rules corrections or changes that are deemed necessary by the Tournament Organizers to maintain the integrity and enjoyment of this event.  
**4.1 Cheating**  
Any player caught cheating may be immediately removed from the tournament and banned from the remainder of the current and/or all future tournaments at the discretion of Tournament Organizers. Cheating includes, but is not limited to,1) behavior that purposefully bypasses the intentions of any rules or guidelines set forth by this document; 2\) any methods that configure the game to unfairly benefit one player over the other; 3\) conspiring between players to achieve desired match or tournament results; 4\) using any type of tool that can read save files or game files in order to gain insight into an opponents game state or otherwise acquire information about your match outside of using the means available to you during a live sessions, 5\) getting outside assistance from others as to the moves of your opponents or the strategies you should use in your current match; 6\) watching live streams or VODs of your current match before it concludes.  
**4.2 Unsporting Conduct**  
Derogatory language, symbols of hatred, racism, anti-semitism, religious intolerance, bigotry, homophobia, gender discrimination, ageism, and ableism will not be tolerated.

Respect how somebody wants to be referred to (e.g. usernames and/or pronouns). If you don't know how somebody wants to be referred to, ask and then follow their request.  
Targeted harassment of any Player, Spectator, Caster, or Organizer is grounds for immediate removal from the tournament. If one player has a personal issue with another player, they will alert the Tournament Organizers, inform them of the issue, and allow them to handle the situation.  
**4.3 Tournament Delays**  
Players must respect the time constraints of all other players. It is understood that Old World is a long game, and that each of us are taking time out of our schedules to ensure the tournament proceeds smoothly. While delays are unavoidable, players are not permitted to intentionally delay or reschedule a match in order to give themselves an edge in an upcoming game. In this vein, “wanting to practice” or “wanting to research” are not acceptable grounds for delays.  
When it comes time for your game to be scheduled, we expect players to abide by the courtesy of scheduling their game with their opponent at their earliest possible convenience.  
**4.4 Outside Assistance**  
Players are not allowed to communicate about strategy or advice with spectators or non-players during or before the conclusion of their game. It is agreed and understood that the nature of this competition consists of 1 on 1 matches between the two players scheduled to face off against each other in the Old World. Any behavior that would disrupt or expand that dynamic to include participants outside of the two players assigned to face each other is prohibited.  
**4.5 Stream Sniping, VOD Watching, Discord**  
Players are not allowed to watch the live stream of their own match or the chat of the spectators. Players are allowed to access the Old World Discord channel during their match, and Tournament Organizers will ensure relevant tournament discussion is limited to non-strategic discussion until after each game has concluded.  
If a spectator reaches out to a player while the game is live, that player should make the Tournament Caster aware and ask the spectator to refrain from game related communication. If a game takes multiple sessions before reaching a conclusion, it is understood and agreed by players that they will **not** view any VODs or content regarding the match they are currently participating in until after it has concluded.

We ask our viewers and spectators for their assistance in ensuring the integrity of each match by avoiding spoilers or attempting to aid one competitor over another.

**4.6 Streamers as Players**  
We encourage our players to stream, however, we would kindly ask that players disable chat features during live tournament play, and to only enable comments on their tournament Videos **after** their match has concluded.
